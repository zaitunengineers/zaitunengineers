Who We Are

A foundry built on molten discipline

Located in the industrial heart of GIDC, Vapi, Zaitun Engineers specialises in the machining of aluminium sand castings and gravity die cast components — a complete cast-to-finish operation serving OEMs across Gujarat and India.

Patterns and core boxes are machined from a single reference on VMC to minimise casting shift — so every component comes out closer to net shape, with tighter tolerances.
