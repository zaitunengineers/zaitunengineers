Common Questions

Before you reach out

Answers to what most buyers ask before sending a drawing. Don't see yours — just WhatsApp us.

What's the difference between sand casting and die casting — which do I need?

Sand casting uses a sand mould and suits lower volumes, larger components, and faster turnaround on new patterns — good for prototypes and running production alike. Gravity die casting uses a reusable metal mould, giving tighter dimensional consistency at higher volumes, but needs tooling lead time upfront. If you're not sure which fits your component, send us the drawing and quantity — we'll recommend the right process.

Do you need a physical sample, or can you work from a drawing alone?

A drawing is enough to quote and produce from — 2D or 3D, with tolerances marked. A physical sample helps for reverse-engineering an existing part if drawings aren't available, but isn't required.

What aluminium grades do you work with?

We commonly work with LM6, LM9, and LM25 aluminium alloys. If your application needs a specific grade, let us know when you send your requirement and we'll confirm feasibility.

What quantities do you take on — prototypes or only bulk orders?

Both. We handle everything from single prototype pieces to running production quantities. Tell us your quantity and timeline and we'll advise on lead time and pricing structure.

What's your typical lead time?

Depends on the component, quantity, and whether new tooling/pattern work is needed. Send your drawing and quantity via WhatsApp or the form below and we'll give you a specific timeline, not a generic estimate.

Do you supply outside Gujarat?

Yes — we dispatch across India. Being based in GIDC, Vapi keeps us well-connected for logistics across Gujarat, Maharashtra, and pan-India shipping.
