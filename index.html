Who We Are

A foundry built on molten discipline

Located in the industrial heart of GIDC, Vapi, Zaitun Engineers specialises in the machining of aluminium sand castings and gravity die cast components. What started as a small foundry floor is today a complete cast-to-finish operation serving OEMs across Gujarat and India.

Patterns and core boxes are machined from a single reference on VMC to minimise casting shift — so every component comes out closer to net shape, with less machining allowance and tighter tolerances.

  • Single-reference VMC-machined patterns & core boxes
  • In-house melting, moulding, casting & machining
  • Lot-wise dimensional inspection & material traceability
  • Supply across Gujarat and pan-India
